## On doing SCQA (and plans) — 2024-09-01

Again and again in planning (e.g. with Boaz) it's clear how valuable it is to actually do an SCQA or a plan and have it done. A huge amount of time is spent "generating" inefficiently in meetings — think of all the recordings where a part would be useful but never gets captured.

An SCQA with a proper question tree lets us see structure, and lets work be allocated to a specific point. Underlying micro-principles:

- **Pyramids** — structure questions and knowledge into pyramids (DAGs). Cf. Minto pyramids.
- **Atomization** (zettelkasten) — splitting information into appropriate atoms so each can be assigned against a given leaf (or leaves) in the tree. The leaves are themselves examples of atomization.
